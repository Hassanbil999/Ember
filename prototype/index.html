How this is calculated

A simple model fitted to two published results: BASS-II limits for acrylic (burns at 17% O₂ in microgravity, not at 18% in normal gravity, no spread below about 0.6 cm/s) and the Saffire-I spread rate of 1.8 mm/s.

Flame limit: Xlim = [Xmin + k·ln²(ueff/u*)]·(14.7/P)0.15, with ueff = √(u² + ub² + ud²) and gravity-driven flow ub ≈ 30√g cm/s. Spread: V = c·(XO₂ − Xlim)·ueff/(ueff+5).

It shows trends. It is not a design tool.
